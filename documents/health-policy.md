# Health Insurance Policy

## Document Information

- Policy Type: Health Insurance
- Product Code: HEALTH-CARE
- Version: 1.0
- Effective Date: 2026-01-01
- Status: Active

## Overview

The Health Insurance Policy provides financial protection for eligible
medical expenses incurred by insured members during the active policy
period.

Benefits are subject to coverage limits, waiting periods, exclusions,
deductibles, and other policy conditions.

## Eligibility

Individuals between 18 and 65 years of age may apply for the policy.

Eligible dependent family members may be included according to the policy
schedule.

Coverage applies only to individuals specifically listed as insured
members.

## Policy Period

The standard policy period is 12 months.

Coverage begins on the effective date specified in the policy schedule and
ends on the stated expiration date unless renewed.

## Hospitalization Coverage

Eligible hospitalization expenses are covered when an insured member is
admitted to a recognized hospital for medically necessary treatment.

Covered expenses may include:

- Room charges.
- Nursing expenses.
- Doctor consultation charges.
- Eligible diagnostic tests.
- Eligible medicines used during hospitalization.
- Operation theatre charges.
- Eligible surgical expenses.

All payments remain subject to the sum insured and policy conditions.

## Minimum Hospitalization

A hospitalization normally requires admission for at least 24 consecutive
hours.

The 24-hour requirement does not apply to eligible day-care procedures
that are specifically recognized under the policy.

## Pre-Hospitalization Expenses

Eligible medical expenses incurred during the 30 days immediately before
an approved hospitalization may be covered when they relate directly to
the condition for which hospitalization occurred.

Supporting bills, prescriptions, and diagnostic reports must be provided.

## Post-Hospitalization Expenses

Eligible medical expenses incurred during the 60 days immediately after
discharge may be covered when they relate directly to the approved
hospitalization.

The policyholder must provide the required medical documents and bills.

## Ambulance Coverage

Emergency road ambulance expenses are covered up to INR 5,000 per approved
hospitalization.

The ambulance service must be medically necessary and related to the
covered hospitalization.

## Waiting Period

A 30-day initial waiting period applies to non-accidental hospitalization
from the policy effective date.

Hospitalization resulting directly from a covered accident is not subject
to this initial 30-day waiting period.

Specific conditions may have separate waiting periods when stated in the
policy schedule.

## Cashless Treatment

Cashless treatment may be available at participating network hospitals.

The insured member should contact the insurer or authorized administrator
for pre-authorization whenever circumstances permit.

Cashless authorization does not automatically mean that every hospital
expense is covered.

Non-covered expenses remain the responsibility of the insured member.

## Reimbursement Claims

When cashless treatment is unavailable, the insured member may submit an
eligible reimbursement claim.

Required documents normally include:

- Completed claim form.
- Hospital discharge summary.
- Final hospital bill.
- Payment receipts.
- Doctor prescriptions.
- Diagnostic reports when applicable.
- Valid identification.
- Bank details for reimbursement.

Additional documents may be requested during claim review.

## Exclusions

The policy does not cover expenses arising solely from:

- Cosmetic procedures that are not medically necessary.
- Experimental treatment not recognized under the policy.
- Treatment obtained for fraudulent purposes.
- Medical services received by a person who is not an insured member.
- Expenses incurred after policy expiration unless specifically covered
  under an approved claim provision.

Other exclusions may apply according to the policy schedule.

## Claim Notification

The insured member should notify the insurer as soon as reasonably
possible for planned or emergency hospitalization.

For reimbursement claims, all required documents should be submitted
through the approved claim channel.

Detailed submission steps are described in the Claim Process document.

## Renewal

The policy may be renewed subject to the terms, premium, and underwriting
rules applicable at the time of renewal.
