# Insurance Claim Process

## Document Information

- Document Type: Claim Process
- Version: 1.0
- Effective Date: 2026-01-01
- Status: Active

## Overview

This document describes the standard process for submitting and tracking
insurance claims.

The exact documents required depend on the insurance product and nature of
the incident.

## Step 1 - Notify the Insurer

The customer should notify the insurer as soon as reasonably possible
after becoming aware of an incident that may result in a claim.

The customer should provide:

- Policy number.
- Name of the insured person or policyholder.
- Date of incident.
- Type of incident.
- Contact information.
- Brief description of the loss.

A claim reference number will be generated after successful claim
registration.

## Step 2 - Collect Supporting Documents

The customer must collect documents relevant to the claim.

For a motor insurance claim, supporting documents may include:

- Motor claim form.
- Vehicle registration certificate.
- Driving licence of the driver.
- Photographs of vehicle damage.
- Repair estimate.
- Police report when applicable.
- Final repair invoice when required.

For a health insurance reimbursement claim, supporting documents may
include:

- Health claim form.
- Hospital discharge summary.
- Final hospital bill.
- Payment receipts.
- Doctor prescriptions.
- Diagnostic reports.
- Valid identification.
- Bank account information.

The insurer may request additional documents depending on the claim.

## Step 3 - Submit the Claim

The customer should submit the claim and supporting documents through an
approved claim channel.

Documents must be readable and complete.

Incomplete submissions may delay claim processing.

The customer should retain copies of all submitted documents.

## Step 4 - Claim Validation

The insurer validates:

- Policy status.
- Coverage period.
- Customer eligibility.
- Incident information.
- Submitted documents.
- Applicable coverage.
- Applicable exclusions.

If required information is missing, the claim may be placed in a pending
state until the customer provides the requested information.

## Step 5 - Claim Assessment

After validation, the claim is assessed against the applicable policy
terms.

The assessment may include:

- Coverage verification.
- Damage assessment.
- Medical bill review.
- Deductible calculation.
- Policy-limit verification.
- Fraud and anomaly checks.

A surveyor, medical reviewer, or other specialist may be assigned when
required.

## Step 6 - Claim Decision

After assessment, the claim may be:

- Approved.
- Partially approved.
- Rejected.
- Kept pending for additional information.

The customer should receive information explaining the claim decision.

## Step 7 - Settlement

For an approved reimbursement claim, payment is made to the registered
bank account according to the approved settlement amount.

For an approved cashless health claim, eligible expenses may be settled
directly with the network hospital.

For an approved motor repair claim, settlement may be made according to
the repair arrangement and policy terms.

## Claim Status

A claim may have one of the following statuses:

- REGISTERED
- DOCUMENTS_PENDING
- UNDER_REVIEW
- APPROVED
- PARTIALLY_APPROVED
- REJECTED
- SETTLED

Customers should use their claim reference number when requesting claim
status.

## Rejected Claims

A claim may be rejected when the loss is not covered by the policy, when
an applicable exclusion applies, or when required claim conditions are
not satisfied.

A rejection should include the reason for the decision.

## Human Review

Customers may request human review when they believe a claim decision
requires further investigation.

Claims involving disputes, unusual circumstances, suspected fraud, or
complex policy interpretation may also be escalated for manual review.

## Fraud Prevention

Customers must provide accurate information and authentic supporting
documents.

Suspected fraudulent claims may be escalated for investigation.

Providing false or manipulated documents may result in claim rejection and
other actions permitted by applicable rules and policy terms.
