# Motor Insurance Policy

## Document Information

- Policy Type: Motor Insurance
- Product Code: MOTOR-PLUS
- Version: 1.0
- Effective Date: 2026-01-01
- Status: Active

## Overview

Motor Insurance Policy provides financial protection for eligible insured
vehicles against covered losses arising from accidents, theft, fire, and
specified natural or man-made events.

Coverage is subject to the policy terms, exclusions, deductibles, limits,
and conditions described in this document.

## Eligibility

The policy is available for privately owned passenger vehicles registered
for personal use.

The vehicle must have a valid registration certificate at the time the
policy becomes effective.

Commercial vehicles are not covered under this policy.

## Policy Period

The standard policy period is 12 months from the policy effective date.

The exact start and end dates are specified in the policy schedule issued
to the customer.

Coverage ends when the policy expires unless the customer renews the
policy.

## Accident Coverage

The policy covers eligible physical damage to the insured vehicle caused
by an accidental collision.

Covered repair costs are subject to the applicable deductible and policy
limits.

The customer must notify the insurer as soon as reasonably possible after
an accident.

## Theft Coverage

Loss of the insured vehicle due to theft is covered when the incident is
reported to the police and the insurer.

The customer must provide a copy of the police report and relevant vehicle
documents when submitting the claim.

The insurer may request additional information during claim investigation.

## Fire Coverage

Damage to the insured vehicle caused by accidental fire is covered,
subject to policy exclusions.

Damage caused intentionally by the policyholder or an authorized driver is
not covered.

## Natural Events

The policy covers eligible vehicle damage caused by specified natural
events including flood, storm, cyclone, earthquake, and landslide.

Coverage remains subject to exclusions and policy limits.

## Deductible

A deductible of INR 2,000 applies to each approved own-damage claim.

The deductible is the amount that the policyholder must pay before the
insurer pays the remaining eligible claim amount.

For example, if an eligible repair costs INR 20,000, the policyholder pays
INR 2,000 and the insurer may pay the remaining INR 18,000, subject to
other policy terms and limits.

## Policy Cancellation

The policyholder may request cancellation within 15 calendar days from the
policy purchase date.

If cancellation is requested within this 15-day period and no claim has
been made, the policyholder is eligible for a full premium refund.

If a claim has already been made, a full premium refund is not available.

Cancellation requests submitted after 15 calendar days may be subject to
cancellation charges and refund calculations defined by the insurer.

## Renewal

The policyholder may renew the policy before its expiration date.

Renewal is subject to the premium, coverage, underwriting rules, and terms
applicable at the time of renewal.

Coverage is not guaranteed after the policy expiration date unless renewal
has been completed.

## Exclusions

The policy does not cover:

- Damage caused intentionally by the policyholder.
- Damage arising from illegal racing.
- Normal wear and tear.
- Mechanical or electrical breakdown not caused by a covered event.
- Loss occurring while the vehicle is used for an unauthorized commercial
  purpose.
- Loss caused while the driver does not hold a legally required valid
  driving licence.

## Claim Notification

Accidents, theft, fire, and other potentially covered losses should be
reported as soon as reasonably possible.

Customers should provide the policy number, vehicle registration number,
incident details, and available supporting documents.

Detailed claim submission requirements are described in the Claim Process
document.

## Customer Support

Customers who cannot resolve a policy or claim question through the
standard support process may request human assistance.

Complex disputes, suspected fraud, and exceptional cases may be escalated
for manual review.

## Roadside Assistance

Emergency roadside assistance may be available according to the benefits
specified in the policy schedule.
