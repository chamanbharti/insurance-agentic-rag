# Insurance Refund Policy

## Document Information

- Document Type: Refund Policy
- Version: 1.0
- Effective Date: 2026-01-01
- Status: Active

## Overview

This document describes general premium refund rules for eligible policy
cancellations.

Product-specific policy terms take precedence when they contain more
specific cancellation or refund conditions.

## Motor Insurance Cancellation Refund

A motor insurance policyholder may request cancellation within 15 calendar
days from the policy purchase date.

If the cancellation request is received within the 15-day period and no
claim has been made, the customer is eligible for a full premium refund.

If a claim has already been made under the policy, the customer is not
eligible for a full premium refund.

Cancellation after the 15-day period may result in cancellation charges
or a reduced refund according to applicable policy terms.

## Refund Request

The customer should provide:

- Policy number.
- Policyholder name.
- Cancellation reason.
- Registered contact details.
- Bank or payment information when required.

The insurer may request additional documents to validate the refund
request.

## Refund Validation

Before approving a refund, the insurer may verify:

- Policy status.
- Policy purchase date.
- Cancellation request date.
- Claim history.
- Premium payment status.
- Customer identity.
- Applicable cancellation rules.

## Refund Method

An approved refund will normally be returned through the original payment
method when technically possible.

If the original payment method cannot be used, the insurer may request
verified bank account details.

## Refund Processing Time

An approved refund is normally processed within 7 business days after all
required cancellation and refund information has been validated.

Banking or payment-provider processing may result in additional time
before the amount appears in the customer's account.

## Duplicate Payments

When a customer has accidentally paid the same premium more than once,
the duplicate payment may be refunded after payment verification.

The valid policy premium remains payable.

## Failed Transactions

If a payment is debited from the customer's account but the policy
purchase is not completed, the transaction should be investigated.

When the payment is confirmed as received but no valid policy was issued,
an eligible refund should be initiated.

## Refund Status

A refund may have one of the following statuses:

- REQUESTED
- UNDER_VALIDATION
- APPROVED
- REJECTED
- PROCESSING
- COMPLETED

## Rejected Refund Requests

A refund request may be rejected when:

- The policy is not eligible for the requested refund.
- A claim has already been made and the requested full refund is therefore
  unavailable.
- Required information cannot be validated.
- The request conflicts with applicable policy cancellation terms.

The customer should receive the reason for a rejected refund request.

## Escalation

Customers may request human assistance when a refund has not been received
after the expected processing period or when they disagree with a refund
decision.

Complex refund disputes may be escalated for manual review.
